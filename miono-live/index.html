Newest · Moonbag

Stop selling too early.

I kept doing the same dumb thing: buying something, watching it run, then selling the whole bag before the real move.

Moonbag turns that behavioral problem into infrastructure. On the buy, it slices off a configurable reserve and moves it to a separate wallet. Later, “sell all” can genuinely mean sell the active bag without accidentally deleting the tail.

BUY → SPLIT → TRADING WALLET + MOONBAG WALLET
normal exit → active bag only
rug emergency → explicitly liquidate both

About

I build systems around how people actually behave.

I keep ending up at the overlap of agents, software, markets, games, and internet culture. I like systems that remember context, cross tool boundaries, and do something real instead of stopping at a prompt box.

That is the through-line between a moonbag guard, a classroom trivia engine, an on-chain game, a research surface, and agent infrastructure: start with an actual friction point, then make the software absorb as much of that friction as possible.

Bushleague is where I ship. Miono is where I explain what the pieces add up to.
